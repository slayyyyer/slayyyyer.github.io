My world

Twenty projects, one small world.

Five districts: the systems on the foundry coast, the languages up on the scriptorium terrace, the games in the arcade, the data projects on the observatory hills, and the motocross valley. Every building opens its project.

Hover or focus a building for a one-line note. On a narrow screen the list comes first; the map is still there, and it scrolls sideways. The arcade keeps the games and the fan-made big top; the three language projects share the terrace above it.

Foundry coast

systems
  • JErlang/jerlang/ An Erlang runtime for the JVM — the engine works.
  • Orders Service/orders-service/ One orders domain, three repos, three docks.
  • Buffy/buffy/ A small cottage with a desk companion in the window.

Scriptorium

language
  • Morphex/morphex/ Deterministic English inflection, Java wing.
  • Transcend/transcend/ English↔French translation, trained from scratch.
  • Why/why-lang/ A page language for the JVM: drop a file, hit its URL, it runs.
  • whyxon render An X clone built from Why pages.
  • j.ts/j/ A J interpreter you can type into.

Arcade quarter

games
  • Yahtzee/yahtzee/ Exact-probability dice, every roll solved.
  • Blackjack Lab/blackjack/ A card table that re-derives its own strategy.
  • Word Chomp!/word-chomp/ A garden word dig against a 2:20 clock.
  • Boggle/boggle/ Sixteen letter dice, every word you can find.
  • The Amazing Digital Circus/tadc/ 33 acts, one big top, no exit.

Observatory hills

data
  • Earth Radio/earth-radio/ One day of earthquakes, played as sound.
  • Charlotte Almanac/clt-almanac/ A daily forecast from the Charlotte record.
  • Murmur/murmur/ A small face that reacts to the weather.
  • What Changed Today/what-changed/ A daily ledger of what actually moved.

Motocross valley

play
  • MX Lab/mx-lab/ Seven seasons of motocross, measured.
  • MotoBuzz/motobuzz/ What the sport is talking about, ranked.
  • SAF Explorer/saf-explorer/ A crate warehouse for .saf archives.

Twenty projects across five districts. Adding a project means one SVG group and one list entry — the map is hand-drawn, not generated.
