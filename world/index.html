My world

Sixteen projects, one small world.

Four regions: the systems on the coast, the games in the arcade, the data projects up on the hills, and the motocross valley. Every building opens its project.

Hover or focus a building for a one-line note. On a narrow screen the list comes first; the map is still there, and it scrolls sideways. Boggle sits at the end of the Arcade quarter.

Map and list of projects

The map is wider than a phone screen. When it is focused, use the arrow keys to scroll sideways, or switch to the list view.

Foundry coast

systems
  • JErlang/jerlang/ An Erlang runtime for the JVM — the engine works.
  • Orders Service/orders-service/ One orders domain, three repos, three docks.
  • Morphex/morphex/ Deterministic English inflection, Java wing.
  • Morphex TS/morphex-ts/ The same library, rebuilt for TypeScript.
  • Buffy/buffy/ A small cottage with a desk pet in the window.

Arcade quarter

games
  • Yahtzee/yahtzee/ Exact-probability dice, every roll solved.
  • Blackjack Lab/blackjack/ A card table that re-derives its own strategy.
  • Word Chomp!/word-chomp/ A garden word dig against a 2:20 clock.
  • Boggle/boggle/ Sixteen letter dice, every word you can find.

Observatory hills

data
  • Earth Radio/earth-radio/ One day of earthquakes, played as sound.
  • Charlotte Almanac/clt-almanac/ A daily forecast from the Charlotte record.
  • Murmur/murmur/ A small face that reacts to the weather.
  • What Changed Today/what-changed/ A daily ledger of what actually moved.

Motocross valley

play
  • MX Lab/mx-lab/ Seven seasons of motocross, measured.
  • MotoBuzz/motobuzz/ What the sport is talking about, ranked.
  • SAF Explorer/saf-explorer/ A crate warehouse for .saf archives.

Sixteen projects across four regions. Adding a project means one SVG group and one list entry — the map is hand-drawn, not generated.
